## decisions documented

- **performance thresholds:** PageStack edges capped at 12 (from 30), FlippedPagesStack at 8 (from 30). deeper elements are fully occluded.

- **content loading:** static-first with async Supabase overlay. no blocking — page renders immediately with static data, swaps when fetch completes.
- **animation timing:** entrance animations 250-400ms with easeOut. reduced motion respected everywhere.
- **cloudinary_url column:** now stores Supabase URLs. column name is a misnomer but works. rename in future migration.
